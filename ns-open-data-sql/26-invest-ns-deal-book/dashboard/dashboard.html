Invest NS deal book

Financial contributions recorded by Invest Nova Scotia across six fiscal years, by sector, county, deal type, and year. Every number on this page is added up in the browser from the exported data below. Nothing here is written in by hand.

Contribution by sector

Sector labels are shown as the province files them. Related spellings such as ICT and ICT (includes Digital Media) are counted separately.

    Contribution by county

    The Halifax family of labels is folded onto one county. Values that do not name a county are kept and marked.

      Deal-type mix by fiscal year

      Each year's dollars split across the deal types recorded that year, with each type's share of its own year. The naming convention moves year to year, which is why a program can appear as an abbreviation in one year and spelled out in another.

      What is counted and what is set aside

      Nothing is dropped. These classes are reported so the totals above can be read for what they are.
