Nova Scotia long-term care bed supply

Licensed nursing home and residential care capacity by zone, facility type, and facility, from the Nova Scotia Open Data portal. Every figure on this page is derived in the browser from the exported dataset below; nothing is hardcoded.

Bed definition: total beds = nursing home beds + residential care beds. Respite beds are counted and shown separately, and stay out of that total.

Beds by zone

Each bar is a zone's total beds, split into nursing home beds and residential care beds. Zones are the four provincial health management zones.
